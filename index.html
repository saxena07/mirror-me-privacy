Mirror Me – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Mirror Me ("the app", "the game") is a puzzle game for Android developed by Lakshya Saxena ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information the game stores on your device

Your level progress, settings and hint count are stored locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to me. The game does not require an account or login.

2. Information collected automatically by third-party services

The app uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows optional rewarded ads that let you earn a hint. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash and performance reporting (such as Sentry or Firebase Crashlytics): collects crash logs, device model, operating system version and app version, to help me find and fix bugs.
  • Google Play services: used for app distribution and, if enabled, Play Games features such as achievements or leaderboards.

I do not receive your name, email address, phone number or precise location from the game.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to run the game, show ads, measure and fix crashes, and improve the app. I do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Users in the European Economic Area and the UK are shown a consent form before personalized ads are served.

5. Children

Mirror Me is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Progress data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. Data held by third-party services is governed by their own policies and retention periods.

7. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information the app handles, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: the developer via Instagram: @mirror.me_play
